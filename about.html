About Blesscon Candy

A growing confectionery business focused on distinctive products, dependable market service and long-term relationships.

Creating products people notice, and businesses can build around.

Blesscon Candy is a confectionery company with a clear focus on product presentation, practical packing and consistent customer service. We build candy that consumers enjoy and that retailers, wholesalers and distribution partners find easy to display and sell.

Our RANI Fruit Candy collection brings seven flavors (Strawberry, Orange, Mango, Peach, Red Grapes, Blueberry and Pineapple) together in one recognizable family. Each box has 60 wrapped candies and 24 boxes make a carton.

Alongside RANI we now offer Fruity Galas Jumbo chewy sweets in nine flavours, Zoombie Sour Chew in five flavours, toffees, energy candies, colourful candy cones (Pan Masala, Sweet Eggs, Sweet Saunf and Mix Pan Masala) and Rani Candy Gift Packs, with wholesale supply available across Pakistan.

We are developing our portfolio for the long term, adding products over time while keeping the brand professional and accessible.

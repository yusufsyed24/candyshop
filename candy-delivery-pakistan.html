Candy Delivery Across Pakistan

Order Blesscon Candy from any city. We ship by cargo or bus and share the builty charges after your order.

Blesscon Candy delivers candy across Pakistan. The minimum order is Rs. 10,000 for Sindh outside Karachi, Rs. 15,000 for Punjab, Islamabad and Balochistan, and Rs. 20,000 for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Azad Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan. Our team shares the builty (freight) charges after you place the order.

Frequently asked questions

Do you deliver candy to Lahore, Islamabad and other cities?

Yes. We ship across Pakistan by cargo or bus. Lahore, Rawalpindi, Islamabad and other cities in Punjab and Islamabad have a Rs. 15,000 minimum order. Builty charges are shared by our team after you place the order.

What is the minimum order outside Karachi?

Rs. 10,000 for Sindh outside Karachi (and Hub), Rs. 15,000 for Punjab, Islamabad and Balochistan, and Rs. 20,000 for Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Azad Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan.

What are builty charges and who shares them?

Builty is the freight (cargo or bus) charge for sending your order to another city. Our team shares the builty charges with you after your order is placed.

Can I mix different candies to reach the minimum?

Yes, any combination of our products counts towards the minimum order.

Candy Delivery in Karachi

Minimum order and delivery charge for every Karachi area, DHA to Surjani.

Candy delivery in Karachi is charged by zone. The minimum candy order runs from Rs. 500 for nearby areas to Rs. 4,000 for outlying areas (Rs. 5,000 beyond 40 km), and delivery costs Rs. 250 to Rs. 1,450. You can mix different candies to reach the minimum.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order for candy delivery in DHA Karachi?

It depends on the phase. Nearby DHA phases (5 to 10 km) have a Rs. 1,000 minimum and Rs. 450 delivery; further DHA phases (10 to 15 km) have a Rs. 1,500 minimum and Rs. 600 delivery. Your final zone is confirmed from your map pin.

What is the minimum order for Clifton?

Clifton is in the 10 to 15 km zone: minimum order Rs. 1,500 and delivery charge Rs. 600.

What is the minimum order for Gulshan-e-Iqbal and Gulistan-e-Johar?

Nearby sections of Gulshan-e-Iqbal are in the 10 to 15 km zone (Rs. 1,500 minimum, Rs. 600 delivery). Gulistan-e-Johar is in the 15 to 20 km zone (Rs. 2,000 minimum, Rs. 750 delivery).

Is the minimum order based on the candy total or the delivery too?

The minimum is checked against the candy subtotal. Delivery is charged separately and is not counted towards the minimum.

Can I mix different candies to reach the minimum?

Yes. You can combine any flavours and products, for example RANI, Fruity Galas and energy candies, to reach your area's minimum order.

My area is not listed. What should I do?

Choose "My area is not listed" at checkout and paste your Google Maps pin. We confirm your zone and delivery charge with you. Until then a Rs. 4,000 minimum applies.

Do you deliver to areas more than 40 km away, like Bahria Town?

Yes, for orders of Rs. 5,000 or more. Our team confirms the route and quotes the delivery charge before the order is confirmed.

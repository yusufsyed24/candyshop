Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on ordering, wholesale, supply and packing.

How do I place an order?

Add your products to the cart on this page, choose boxes/pouches or full cartons, and press Place Order. Your order goes to our team on WhatsApp or email. You can also message us on WhatsApp with your Name, Shop/Business Name, Area, Product Name and Required Quantity, and our team will process your request.

Do you supply wholesale?

Yes, wholesale supply is available. Use the Wholesale page to choose your state and city and list the cartons you need, or share the product name and required quantity, and our Sales Team will assist you with wholesale rates and bulk orders.

Can I buy in retail quantities?

Yes, retail purchase is available. Send us your location and the product you need, and our team will guide you on availability.

I want to supply or distribute Blesscon Candy in my area. What should I do?

Thank you for your interest. Please send your Name, Business/Shop Name, Area, Contact Number and the products you need. Our Sales Team will contact you. You can use the Business Enquiries form on the Contact page or message us on WhatsApp.

How many candies are in a box, and how many boxes in a carton?

It depends on the product. The packing details for every product are listed in the table below. RANI Fruit Candy is 60 candies per box and 24 boxes per carton. Ask our team if you need packing details for a specific item.

What are your prices?

Box/pouch and carton prices are shown on each product card. For the latest wholesale, retail, box and carton rates, please contact our Sales Team, as prices may vary according to product and order quantity.

Questions about custom orders

Quick answers before you send your request.

Can I put our names, a date or a hashtag on the label?

Yes. Type the main text and a small line in the designer and you will see them on the label straight away. After you send your order, share your logo or exact spelling on WhatsApp and our team will confirm the final label with you.

How many packs should I order?

Enter your guest count plus a few extra for surprise guests. Our team confirms quantity, price and delivery for your event date.

What does it cost?

Candy gift bags are estimated at Rs. 45 per bag. Favour cones are quoted by our team because labels, ribbons and fillings vary. The final price is always confirmed before you pay.

Can I mix flavours?

Yes. Choose as many flavours as you like from RANI Fruit Candy and Fruity Galas Jumbo, or let our team suggest a mix. We confirm what fits your pack.

Is anything charged online?

No. Your design opens in WhatsApp or your email app with every detail filled in. Our team replies with price, design and delivery details.

My event date is close. What should I do?

Add your event date in the form and send your request as early as you can. Our team will tell you whether your date can be met.

Not sure what to pick?

Tell us about your event and our team will suggest packs, flavours and quantities.
